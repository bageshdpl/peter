PETER FOR WINDOWS (ready to run, no installation)
=================================================

1. Start Peter
   Double-click  start-peter.cmd
   A black window opens (keep it open) and Chrome opens Peter at
   http://localhost:5173. Create an account: it lives only on this computer.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
   then "Run anyway". (To avoid this next time: before unzipping, right-click
   the zip file, choose Properties, tick "Unblock", click OK.)

2. Add the Peter Observer extension to Chrome (once)
   - Go to chrome://extensions
   - Turn on "Developer mode" (top right)
   - Click "Load unpacked" and choose the  extension  folder in this folder
   - Pin "Peter Observer" from the puzzle-piece menu

3. Turn on AI drafting (optional)
   Follow the steps in  my-keys.example.cmd  then start Peter again.
   Without keys, Peter offers "Sample draft (no AI)".

4. Stop Peter
   Close the black window.

Your data
   Everything is saved in this folder: peter.db and the peter-data folder.
   Back up those two to keep your work. Delete them to start fresh.

Updating
   Download the new zip, unzip it to a new folder, and copy peter.db and
   peter-data from the old folder into the new one before starting.
