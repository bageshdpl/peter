Ready-to-run Peter for Windows. No installation and no admin rights needed.

1. Download the zip below. Before unzipping, right-click it, choose Properties, tick **Unblock**, click OK.
2. Unzip it anywhere in your user folder, for example Documents.
3. Double-click **start-peter.cmd**. Peter opens in your browser at http://localhost:5173.

Read **README-FIRST.txt** in the zip for the Chrome extension and AI keys.
